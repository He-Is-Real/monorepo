# Sign-up consent, version 1 (development placeholder wording)

> Placeholder wording for the development site (D75). The lawyer-checked wording replaces it in
> a later version, before real people sign up.

Before you use your account, we need two things from you. Nothing personal is stored until both
boxes are ticked.

Read the privacy document first: it says what we keep, why, who helps us process it, how long
we keep it, and your rights.

## The boxes

- **`age_18plus`**: I am 18 or over.
- **`account_data`**: Store my account and the stories I save as favourites. I understand that
  saving stories on a Christian testimony site can say something about my beliefs, and I agree
  to He Is Real Today keeping that, as the privacy document describes.

You can withdraw this consent at any time from your account. Withdrawing it deletes your
account.
