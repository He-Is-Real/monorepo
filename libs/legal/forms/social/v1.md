# Sharing your story beyond heisreal.today, version 1 (development placeholder wording)

> Placeholder wording for the development site (D75). The lawyer-checked wording replaces it in
> a later version. How widely the real wording may reach is still being decided: consent to
> share what a story says about your faith has to be specific and informed, so every use is
> named below.

This form is optional. Saying no changes nothing about your story on heisreal.today.

If you agree, He Is Real Today's media team may share your published story, with the name you
chose to show, in the ways listed here, and only these. Anything not listed, including any
platform not named, is not allowed: if we want to add one, we will ask you with a new version of
this form, and only the people who agree to it are included.

## The box

- **`share_socials`**: Let He Is Real Today's media team share my published story in the ways
  this form lists.

## Where

He Is Real Today's own accounts on:

- YouTube;
- Instagram;
- TikTok;
- Facebook.

## How

- **Edited and clipped**: shorter clips, still images, captions, subtitles and translations, as
  long as they don't change what you meant.
- **Partner churches and ministries**: given to churches and Christian ministries we work with,
  for their own websites, social media accounts and services.
- **Events and print**: shown at events and church services, and in printed material such as
  leaflets and posters.
- **Paid promotion**: used in paid adverts that promote He Is Real Today on the platforms above.

## Withdrawing

You can withdraw this consent at any time from your account. From then on we share nothing new,
and within 30 days we remove your story from He Is Real Today's own accounts and stop any paid
promotion that uses it. Your story stays on heisreal.today as long as you consent to that.

We can't control copies others have made: posts others have reshared or saved, what partner
churches and ministries have published on their own channels, and material already printed or
handed out.
