# Saving and sending

Who: Signed-in person, Moderator, Admin.
Data: none.
Next id: REQ-SAV-004

- **REQ-SAV-001** When a person saves something that changed since they opened it, the page reads it again, applies their change if nothing they touched has changed, and asks them only when the two changes really clash. [D70]
- **REQ-SAV-002** A person sees their own change as soon as it is saved. [D25]
- **REQ-SAV-003** When the same save is sent twice, by a double tap or a retry, it is applied once. [D29]
