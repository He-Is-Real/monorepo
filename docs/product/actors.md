# Actors

Who acts on the site. Rules name actors with exactly these words. Words that must not be used for them are in `glossary.md`.

- **Visitor**: someone not signed in, who wants to read, watch and listen to public stories, which needs no account.
- **Signed-in person**: someone with an account, 18 or over, who wants to share their stories, heart stories and look after their own account and data.
- **Moderator**: a staff member with a verified `@heisreal.today` email, who wants to review every story not on the site (except drafts never sent): approve, send back, reject, take down and show again.
- **Admin**: a staff member with a verified `@heisreal.today` email, who does everything a Moderator does, plus drafts never sent, deleting a story, themes, submission limits, legal texts, "approve anyway", the block list and the Staff page.
- **the system**: the site's own services and jobs (api-public-read, api-user, api-admin, the media worker, the nightly clean-up), acting for no person, for example hiding a story while its video can't play.
